## 📊 GOOGLE ANALYTICS - Simple Setup

### What it does:
- Tracks visitors to your site
- Tells you which pages people visit
- Totally free

### Steps (10 minutes):

1. **Go to:** analytics.google.com
2. **Click:** "Start for free" (blue button)
3. **Sign in with:** Your Google account
4. **Click:** "Start measuring" (bottom right)
5. **Account name:** Type "Brandverse"
6. **Property name:** Type "Brandverse Website"
7. **Website URL:** Type "https://brandverse.tech"
8. **Industry category:** Select "Technology"
9. **Business size:** Select "Small"
10. **Click:** "Create"
11. **Copy:** Measurement ID (looks like: G-XXXXXXXXXX)
12. **Paste it here:** Give me the ID, I'll add it to your site

### Done! You'll see visitor data in your analytics dashboard.
