# Secret Shopper Call Tracking System

## Purpose
Track and record results of 8 PM call tests to identify revenue leakage in San Antonio electrical contractors' phone systems.

## Testing Protocol

### Call Schedule
- **Time**: 8:00 PM CST (after business hours)
- **Days**: Monday - Thursday (to avoid weekend patterns)
- **Method**: Call main business line and 24/7 emergency line if separate

### Call Script
```
"Hi, I'm calling because I have an electrical emergency at my home/business. 
I have [power outage/sparking/outlet not working]. 
Can someone help me tonight or first thing tomorrow morning?"
```

### Recording Criteria

#### ✅ POSITIVE OUTCOMES
- Human answers within 3 rings
- Clear emergency handling process
- Specific callback time given
- Professional phone presence

#### ❌ NEGATIVE OUTCOMES  
- Goes to generic voicemail
- No emergency line available
- Voicemail doesn't mention 24/7 service
- Callback time unclear
- Full mailbox

## Quick Reference: Revenue Calculation

**Formula**: 
`(Missed calls/day) × (Average job value) × 30 days = Monthly revenue loss`

**Example**:
5 missed calls/day × $200 average job × 30 days = $30,000/month loss

**Electrical Industry Averages**:
- Average emergency service call: $250-$500
- Average commercial job: $500-$2,000
- Typical missed call rate (without 24/7): 15-25%
