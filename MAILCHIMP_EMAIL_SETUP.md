# Mailchimp Email Sequence Setup Guide

## Overview
This guide will help you set up two email sequences:
1. **Incoming (Auto-reply)** - Immediate response when someone fills out a form
2. **Outgoing (Nurture)** - 5-email sequence to convert leads over time

## Incoming Sequence (Auto-reply)

### Set up in Mailchimp:

1. **Go to** Audience → Settings → Audience name and defaults
2. **Scroll to** "Response emails" section
3. **Edit** "Final welcome email" (this sends immediately when someone joins)

2. **Select** "Email automation" (if available) or "Classic automation"
3. **Choose** "Welcome email series" or "Custom journey"
4. **Set trigger**: "Joins audience" (all contacts)

## Alternative: Use Mailchimp's Pre-built Templates

If you prefer, Mailchimp has pre-built email sequences you can customize:

1. **Go to** Audience → Create Journey
2. **Choose** a template like "Welcome series" or "Lead nurture"
3. **Customize** the content with the templates above
4. **Set timing** (immediate, 2 days, 5 days, 8 days, 12 days)

---

## Testing Your Sequences

1. **Test the auto-reply:** Fill out one of your forms and check if you get the welcome email
2. **Test the nurture sequence:** Add a test email to your audience manually and watch the emails arrive
3. **Monitor performance:** Check Mailchimp's analytics (open rates, click rates, conversions)
