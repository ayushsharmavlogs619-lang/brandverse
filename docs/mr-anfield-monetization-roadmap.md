# Mr Anfield Football — Monetization Roadmap

**Goal:** Build multiple legitimate revenue streams around original Liverpool/football reporting, match analysis, and the Mr Anfield Football YouTube channel.
**Status:** Plan only. No monetization accounts or ad placements have been created by this document.
**Important:** The live production website/repository still needs to be confirmed before code changes are made. Candidate repositories found include `mr-anfield-official` (Next.js, metadata currently uses `mr-anfield.vercel.app`) and `mr-anfield-football` (older static site).

## Revenue streams, in recommended order

### 1. Google AdSense (website display advertising)
**Priority: High, after the site is stable on the new domain**
- Audit original content quality, navigation, mobile layout, site ownership, privacy policy, contact page, About page, and any required consent disclosures.
- Confirm the production site is accessible, crawls correctly, and has no broken pages or placeholder copy.
- Apply using the official AdSense account; add the verification code to the correct live site and submit for review.
- Only after approval, enable ads and test placements. Do not click your own ads or ask anyone to click them.
- Expect review to take time and approval is not guaranteed. Avoid promising a revenue figure before traffic exists.
Official eligibility: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724

### 2. Affiliate links for football products
**Priority: High, can start while building traffic**
- Research official/legitimate affiliate programs that accept publishers from India and permit the traffic sources we intend to use.
- Product categories: football shirts/kits, boots, training equipment, balls, books, gaming/football merchandise and other genuinely relevant products.
- Add contextual links to relevant articles and a clearly labelled recommendations page; disclose affiliate relationships near links.
- Track clicks and approved commissions. Never fabricate discounts, product claims or availability.

### 3. Indoleads CPA/CPL/CPS offers
**Priority: Medium, after publisher account approval**
- Publisher registration is free according to the network's publisher FAQ; approval/moderation and offer terms apply.
- Add the website and YouTube channel honestly as traffic sources.
- Choose only offers relevant to football fans and accepted for the actual audience GEO and traffic type.
- Read allowed/forbidden traffic rules, payout threshold, conversion hold period, caps and cookie window before promoting.
- Never use misleading offers, spam, incentivized actions where prohibited, or paid traffic unless the specific offer allows it.
Publisher info: https://indoleads.com/affiliates/
Publisher FAQ: https://indoleads.com/faqs/

### 4. YouTube Shopping affiliate program
**Priority: Medium, subject to channel eligibility**
- Check YouTube Studio → Earn for current eligibility and available Shopping features.
- If eligible, tag relevant football products in videos/Shorts/live content where available and allowed.
- Keep recommendations honest and relevant. Do not assume the website automatically qualifies the channel.
Official India announcement: https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/youtube-shopping-affiliate-program-arrives-in-india/

### 5. Direct sponsorships and paid placements
**Priority: Later, after audience and analytics data exist**
- Build a one-page media kit with real monthly visitors, page views, audience geography, YouTube views and engagement.
- Approach legitimate football shops, fan merchandise businesses, football apps, local academies, podcasts and sports creators.
- Clearly label sponsored posts and ads. Keep editorial coverage independent and do not sell undisclosed links or fake reviews.

### 6. Newsletter sponsorships / community
**Priority: Later**
- Add an opt-in football newsletter only when we can maintain a consistent schedule and handle consent/unsubscribe properly.
- Build recurring audience through original match previews, tactical analysis, transfer explainers and post-match reaction.
- Offer newsletter sponsorships only after list size and engagement are measurable.

### 7. Optional later experiments
- YouTube memberships / viewer support if channel eligibility and audience demand make sense.
- Digital products such as tactical guides or match-day planners only if we can create genuinely useful original material.
- Avoid betting affiliate offers as an initial monetization route; legal, trust and audience-safety risks are not worth making them a core strategy.

## 30-day execution sequence

### Phase 1 — Foundation (domain launch)
1. Identify the production Mr Anfield project.
2. Connect and verify the new domain, HTTPS, canonical URL, sitemap and redirects.
3. Audit core pages and fix broken navigation/metadata.
4. Verify GA4 and Search Console.

### Phase 2 — AdSense readiness
1. Review every existing article for originality, accuracy, sourcing and reader value.
2. Ensure About, Contact and Privacy pages are complete.
3. Check site mobile performance and crawlability.
4. Apply for AdSense and wait for the decision; do not add fake traffic or click ads.

### Phase 3 — Affiliate pilot
1. Select 1–2 legitimate programs that accept our publisher location and audience.
2. Publish a useful, clearly disclosed football product guide.
3. Add contextual links only where useful.
4. Review click-throughs and approved conversions weekly.

### Phase 4 — Distribution and sponsorship readiness
1. Link articles to relevant YouTube videos and vice versa.
2. Publish original match previews, tactical explainers, transfer analysis and post-match reaction consistently.
3. Build a media kit from actual analytics after enough data exists.
4. Contact a small list of relevant potential sponsors with honest metrics.

## Success metrics
- Indexed useful pages
- Organic search clicks and impressions
- Engaged sessions and returning visitors
- Affiliate outbound clicks and approved conversions
- AdSense application/approval status and policy compliance
- Newsletter opt-ins and unsubscribe rate
- Sponsor leads and revenue actually received

## Guardrails
- No guaranteed income claims; traffic and revenue are uncertain.
- Do not mass-publish shallow AI summaries of other outlets. Google recommends original, helpful, people-first content.
- Disclose affiliate and sponsored relationships clearly.
- Do not copy live match footage or commentary; use licensed/original assets.
- Do not add ad code or tracking IDs to a guessed repository or production host.
